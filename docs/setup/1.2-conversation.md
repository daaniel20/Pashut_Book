# Task 1.2: The Hebrew conversation with buttons

> ✅ **Done on Nov 22, 2026.** Walked through the whole conversation on the owner's phone.

**Time:** about 15 minutes. **Cost:** ₪0.
**Do it on:** the Mac Mini, with your phone nearby.

**What you get at the end:** on the test number, the bot walks you through the whole order:

1. **Welcome** + how it works
2. **Choose a design:** buttons "קלאסי" / "צבעוני"
3. **How to send in HD** + "send your photos"
4. **Photos:** the bot answers once ("קיבלנו!") with a "סיימתי לשלוח ✅" button, then stays quiet while you send the rest
5. **Cover title:** buttons "כן, להוסיף" / "בלי כותרת". If yes, you type the title (up to 40 letters).
6. **Summary:** design, number of photos, title
7. **Changing your mind** (added after the first phone tests; changed in task 1.2b): the summary has **no buttons**. It says the album is being designed and the link comes soon. Changes are made after the link: **✏️ לשנות משהו** opens a list: change title, change design, change cover photo, add photos, **↩️ בלי שינוי**. The cover-title question also has a **📸 להוסיף תמונות** button, for when "done" was tapped too early. After a change, the customer gets an updated summary and the album is built again.
8. **"Done" tapped too early:** with only the cover photo, the bot asks for the rest of the photos. With fewer than 40 photos (`FEW_PHOTOS` in `server/conversation.py`), it asks once: **📸 להוסיף תמונות** or **להמשיך ככה**.
9. **Starting over:** messages with fewer than 3 buttons get a **🔄 להתחיל מחדש** button (was "אלבום חדש" until task 1.2b) once there's something to lose (it's also in the **✏️ לשנות משהו** list, and typing "אלבום חדש" works anywhere). It always asks first: **[כן, להתחיל מחדש] [לא, להמשיך]**. A finished album is kept; an unfinished one is deleted with its photos.
10. **Many HD photos still uploading** (owner's test, Nov 24, 2026): if "סיימתי לשלוח" is tapped while photos are still arriving, the bot says "רגע, מוודאים שכל התמונות הגיעו… ⏳" and goes on only after no photo came for 30 seconds (`QUIET_SECONDS` in `server/orders.py`), so the count is right. A photo that arrives after the summary is added and the album is built again ("קיבלנו עוד תמונות 👍"). WhatsApp's "album" bundle (logged as `unsupported`) gets no answer; a video gets "סרטונים לא נכנסים לאלבום".
11. **The "done" button comes after the photos** (owner's test, Nov 24, 2026): the "send the rest of the photos" message has no "done" button. One minute after the last photo arrives (`QUIET_SECONDS` = 60 in `server/orders.py`), the bot sends "קיבלנו X תמונות 👍 רוצים לשלוח עוד?" with **[סיימתי לשלוח ✅]**, at the bottom of the chat. With fewer than 40 photos the same message says "מומלץ לפחות 40", so "done" doesn't ask again. More photos later → an updated count, again a minute after they stop.
12. **HD question:** **"איך שולחים ב-HD?"** only shows how (with the video clip) and **[להמשיך בלי HD]**; nothing is deleted. The old photos are replaced only when the first new photo arrives.
13. **HD video clip** (`server/media/hd-how-to-iphone.mp4`): goes on top of the HD instructions (when photos are first asked for, in the "how?" answer, and when the cover photo came without HD). For now the iPhone clip is used for everyone.

> **Good to know (for now)**
> - (Task 1.3 now saves the photos and remembers each customer after a restart. See `1.3-photos.md`.)
> - To start over at any time, write: **אלבום חדש**
> - Limit: 100 photos per album (easy to change: `MAX_PHOTOS` in `server/conversation.py`).

> **Words you'll see**
> - **Interactive buttons:** WhatsApp's tap-to-answer buttons. Up to 3 per message, each up to 20 letters.

## Step 1: Try it in the Terminal first (optional, 3 min)

You can walk through the conversation on the Mac with no phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

```bash
cd ~/pashut-book && git pull && python3 server/try_conversation.py
```

Type like a customer. Type `1` or `2` to tap a button, `/photo 20` to send 20 photos, `/quit` to stop.

## Step 3: Walk through it on your phone (10 min)

1. Write **אלבום חדש** (or anything) to the test number.
2. Tap a design, send a few photos **in HD**, tap **סיימתי לשלוח ✅**, add a title.
3. Try a few "mistakes" too: write text instead of tapping, tap "done" before sending photos, send a video.

## The HD video clip

The clip is the owner's own screen recording (iPhone: Control Center → screen recording), showing **only objects, never people or children**, because every customer sees it. It was prepared with `ffmpeg` (a free video tool):
- cut to the useful part, the phone's status bar (time, battery, red recording dot) cut off the top,
- a red circle, an arrow and "לחצו כאן" drawn on the HD button while it's tapped, with a 1.5-second pause just before the tap,
- a **title card** at the start, shown 1.5 seconds (`server/media/hd-how-to-title-card.png`: "ככה שולחים תמונות באיכות HD", the real HD button circled, "סרטון קצר, 11 שניות"). WhatsApp always uses a video's first frame as its preview picture, and without the card that was a confusing screenshot of the chat. The middle of the card is left empty because WhatsApp puts its play button there. **Later: add the logo to the card** once there is one (see ROADMAP.md),
- it ends once a few photos are selected (the sending part was cut: everyone knows how to tap send), holding the last frame 0.7 seconds,
- 720 px wide, H.264, no sound, 11 seconds, about 0.6 MB (WhatsApp allows up to 16 MB).

To replace it, put the new file at the same path and restart the bot; it's uploaded to Meta again by itself. An Android clip can be prepared the same way; then we decide whether one clip is enough for everyone or the bot asks "iPhone or Android?".

## Task 1.2b: users' feedback (Nov 25, 2026)

The owner collected feedback from family members who tried the bot. What changed:

| Feedback | What we did |
|---|---|
| The welcome is long | Shorter, a line break after "שלום!", stresses simple and fast. The "type אלבום חדש" line is gone (the button does it) |
| "🔄 אלבום חדש" is unclear | Now **"🔄 להתחיל מחדש"** (WhatsApp allows 20 characters per button, so "ביטול והתחלה מחדש" + icon didn't fit). Typing "אלבום חדש" still works |
| A "videos aren't used" message, then silence: "is it stuck?" | A video sent with the photos isn't answered on its own. The photo count message says it in its second line: "🎬 סרטונים לא נכנסים לאלבום, רק תמונות." (A video sent on its own, with no photos arriving, still gets its own message) |
| Why 40–100 photos? | "(מומלץ 40 עד 100, כדי שהאלבום ייצא מלא ומגוון)". No numbers per page, because full-page photos would raise questions |
| The cover tip isn't practical | "תמונה לאורך (עומדת) ממלאת את כל הכריכה. תמונה לרוחב תיחתך בצדדים או תופיע קטנה יותר." |
| "כתבו כאן את הכותרת" is confusing | Says it goes on the cover, with two examples. Only the "בלי כותרת" button (no "add photos" here any more) |
| "פשוט כתבו לנו": write what? | Removed (the bot can't understand free text yet, task 3.2). A "✏️ לשנות משהו" button instead |
| The HD question came after the summary, and had no clear "yes" | It now comes **with the photo count**, once the photos stop arriving (see below). Buttons "📸 לשלוח שוב ב-HD" (the how-to with the clip) and "להמשיך בלי HD" (back to the photos, with "סיימתי לשלוח") |
| The album page could say more | It now also shows the average photos per page, how many photos got a whole page, and what was left out and why |

**How the early HD question works:** when a photo is saved, the bot reads its size from the file's first bytes (built-in Python, no extra tools; only the number of pixels is kept, in memory). Without HD, WhatsApp sends photos exactly 2048 pixels on the long side. When the photos stop arriving (no new photo for 60 seconds), the bot waits up to 20 seconds for the last ones to save, and if at least half of them are exactly 2048, the count message becomes "קיבלנו 50 תמונות 👍 … שמנו לב שרובן נשלחו בלי HD …". It asks once per album. If the sizes weren't known in time (at least 3 are needed), the album check still asks after the summary, as before.

Try it without a phone: `python3 server/try_conversation.py`, send `/photo 10`, then type `/wait nohd`.

**Ideas we compared:** a competitor (Negba) asks the customer to type "סיום" when done.
