# Task 1.2: The Hebrew conversation with buttons

> ✅ **Done on Nov 22, 2026.** Walked through the whole conversation on the owner's phone.

**Time:** about 15 minutes. **Cost:** ₪0.
**Do it on:** the Mac Mini, with your phone nearby.

**What you get at the end:** on the test number, the bot walks you through the whole order:

1. **Welcome** + how it works
2. **Choose a design:** buttons "קלאסי" / "צבעוני"
3. **How to send in HD** + "send your photos"
4. **Photos:** the bot answers once ("קיבלנו!") with a "סיימתי לשלוח ✅" button, then stays quiet while you send the rest
5. **Cover title:** buttons "כן, להוסיף" / "בלי כותרת". If yes, you type the title (up to 40 letters).
6. **Summary:** design, number of photos, title
7. **Changing your mind** (added after the first phone tests): the summary has one **✏️ לשנות משהו** button that opens a list: change title, change design, change cover photo, add photos. The cover-title question also has a **📸 להוסיף תמונות** button, for when "done" was tapped too early. After a change, the customer gets an updated summary and the album is built again.

> **Good to know (for now)**
> - (Task 1.3 now saves the photos and remembers each customer after a restart. See `1.3-photos.md`.)
> - To start over at any time, write: **אלבום חדש**
> - Limit: 100 photos per album (easy to change: `MAX_PHOTOS` in `server/conversation.py`).

> **Words you'll see**
> - **Interactive buttons:** WhatsApp's tap-to-answer buttons. Up to 3 per message, each up to 20 letters.

## Step 1: Try it in the Terminal first (optional, 3 min)

You can walk through the conversation on the Mac with no phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

```bash
cd ~/pashut-book && git pull && python3 server/try_conversation.py
```

Type like a customer. Type `1` or `2` to tap a button, `/photo 20` to send 20 photos, `/quit` to stop.

## Step 2: Restart the bot with the new code (2 min)

```bash
cd ~/pashut-book && bash deploy/mac/install-bot-service.sh
```

It asks for your Mac password once.

## Step 3: Walk through it on your phone (10 min)

1. Write **אלבום חדש** (or anything) to the test number.
2. Tap a design, send a few photos **in HD**, tap **סיימתי לשלוח ✅**, add a title.
3. Try a few "mistakes" too: write text instead of tapping, tap "done" before sending photos, send a video.

## Changing the wording

All the bot's Hebrew texts are in `server/texts/he.json`. You can ask Claude to change any of them. After a change, run Step 2 again.
