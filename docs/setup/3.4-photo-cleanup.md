# Task 3.4: Photo cleanup (old photos delete themselves)

**Time:** about 10 minutes. **Cost:** ₪0.
**Do it on:** the Mac Mini.

**Why:** customers' photos are private, often of children (CLAUDE.md rule 5), and Supabase's free plan holds only **1 GB** (rule 11). So the bot now deletes photos by itself once they're no longer needed.

## What gets deleted, and when

| Album | Photos deleted | What happens to the order |
|---|---|---|
| **Not paid** (any step before payment) | **14 days** after the customer's last message | Closed. If the customer writes again, they get: "האלבום הקודם שלכם נמחק 🔒 … נמחק אחרי 14 ימים", then a new album starts as usual. |
| **Paid** | **30 days** after you tapped "payment received" | Stays "paid". (There's no "delivered" step yet, so 30 days leaves time to print, ship, and reprint if something went wrong.) |
| **Cancelled** (the customer started over) | about **an hour** later | Already happened after a minute before; this catches the ones missed if the bot restarted at that moment. |

Each time, **everything with photos in it** goes: the photo files in Supabase, the album folder on the Mac (`output/albums/<order>/`: pages, previews, print PDFs) and its preview link. The order's row and its history stay (they have no photos), marked `photos_deleted`, plus one history row `photos_deleted` (how many files, why, after how many days).

Also deleted: preview links that expired, and the photo check report (`output/quality-report.html`, it has small copies of photos) once it's 14 days old.

**How:** the bot checks a few minutes after it starts, then every 6 hours. Nothing for you to do. Each deletion is one line in the log:
`Cleanup: deleted 57 photos of order 3fa2b1c8 (unpaid, after 15 days)`

> **Words you'll see**
> - **Dry run:** the tool only *shows* what it would delete, and deletes nothing.

Your phone number then starts a fresh album at its next message (with the "deleted" message, which is a good test of it).

## Good to know

- **Changing the days:** `UNPAID_DAYS` and `PAID_DAYS` at the top of `server/cleanup.py`. The customer's message uses the same number.
- **Storage math:** an HD photo is about 1–1.5 MB, so one album is ~60–150 MB and 1 GB holds only **about 7–10 albums at once**. With 14 days for unpaid albums this is fine for the first customers, but around 15+ albums a month it will fill up. Options then: Supabase Pro ($25/month, 100 GB), or keeping the photos on the Mac only. We'll decide when the numbers get close; `python3 server/cleanup.py` shows the current size.
- **Your order history keeps everything else**: the numbers of a deleted album stay in `order_events` / `order_summary` for your study (rule 10).

Code: `server/cleanup.py`; tests: `tests/test_cleanup.py`.
