# Task 3.2: Photo numbers, replace or remove a photo, address and payment

**Time:** about 15 minutes. **Cost:** ₪0.
**Do it on:** Supabase (in the browser), the Mac Mini and your phone.

**Why:** a full order should run from start to finish: the customer can fix a photo they don't like, sends a shipping address, and pays.

**What you get at the end:**
1. **A small number on every photo** in the preview page (a dark circle in the photo's top corner). It is the photo's own number, the order it was sent in, so it never changes when the album is built again. The cover has no number. 🔒 The numbers are on the preview only, **never printed**.
2. **"📷 להחליף/להסיר תמונה"** at the top of the **✏️ לשנות משהו** list (only once the customer has seen the numbers):
   - "איזו תמונה?" → the customer types the number (e.g. `15`). A number that isn't in the album gets "לא מצאנו…".
   - **[📷 להחליף] [🗑️ להסיר] [להשאיר כמו שהיה]**
   - **Replace** → "send the new photo" → it goes into **the same spot**; the rest of the album stays exactly as it was.
   - **Remove** → the album is planned again without it.
   - Then the usual summary, and the new album link comes.
   - At most **10** photo changes per album. After that: "write to us what to change", and their message comes to you.
3. **After you tap ✅ לאשר:**
   - the customer gets "מוכן להדפסה! לאן לשלוח אותו?" and types name, address, city and phone **in one message**,
   - the bot shows it back: **[✅ נכון] [✏️ לתקן]**,
   - **✅ נכון** → the customer sees the price ("₪180 (אלבום ₪150 + משלוח ₪30)") and "we'll send you a Bit payment request to this WhatsApp number",
   - **you** get 🔧 "לגבות תשלום" with the amount, the customer's **full** phone number and the address, and a **[💰 התקבל תשלום]** button.
4. **You send the Bit request by hand**, from your own Bit app, to that number. When the money arrives, tap **💰 התקבל תשלום** → the customer gets "התשלום התקבל… האלבום יוצא להדפסה", and you get the print files' folder.
5. After approval the customer **can't change the album by themselves** any more: "change something" asks them to write what they want, and it comes to you.

> **Words you'll see**
> - **Column:** one piece of information in every row of a Supabase table (like a column in a spreadsheet). This task adds three to `orders`.
> - **Status:** the order's stage in Supabase: `collecting` → `ready` (album built) → `approved` (you approved: address and payment) → `paid`. Or `cancelled`.

## Step 1: Add the new columns in Supabase

The address, the price and the time of payment get their own columns, so you can see them in the table and download them.

1. Open https://supabase.com/dashboard → project **pashut-book** → **SQL Editor** (left menu) → **New query**.
2. Open the file `supabase/3.2-address-and-payment.sql` from the project (on GitHub or on the Mac), copy **all** of it, paste it, and click **Run**.
3. It should say **Success. No rows returned**.

> Running it twice does no harm.

## Step 4: Try it on your phone

Your phone can be both the customer and the owner (as in task 3.1).

1. Make a small album and open the preview link. Every photo inside has a small number.
2. Tap **✏️ לשנות משהו** → **📷 להחליף/להסיר תמונה** → type a number from the preview → **📷 להחליף** → send a new photo. In the new link, the new photo is in the same place (with its own new number), and the other pages didn't change.
3. Do it again with **🗑️ להסיר**: that photo is gone.
4. Tap **😍 אהבתי!**, then **✅ לאשר** on the 🔧 request.
5. As the customer, type an address, e.g. `ישראל ישראלי, הרצל 1, תל אביב, 050-0000000`, then **✅ נכון**.
6. You get 🔧 **לגבות תשלום** with ₪180 and your number. (In a real order: send the Bit request now.) Tap **💰 התקבל תשלום**.
7. In Supabase → **Table Editor** → `orders`: the newest row has `status` = `paid`, the address, `price` = 180 and `paid_at`.

To see what happened: `grep -i "paid\|payment\|album" ~/Library/Logs/pashut-book/bot.log | tail -10`

Try it without a phone too: `python3 server/try_conversation.py` (new commands: `/previews`, `/approve`, `/paid`).

## Good to know

- **The 24-hour window** (see guide 3.1): the "approved" message, the payment request and "payment received" reach the customer only within 24 hours of their last message. Approve, and collect, within a day. Typing **אישורים** now also resends orders waiting for payment.
- **Why Bit by hand:** no payment company yet (no business registration, fees). A payment link (Grow / PayPlus / Cardcom) comes in Stage 4. The customer never types card details in WhatsApp.
- **Privacy:** the address is kept in Supabase only (for shipping), never in the bot log. Delete it with the order's photos later (task 3.4 deletes photos after delivery).
- **The customer's photo changes** are in the order's notes: `removed:15` (photo 15 left out) and `replaced:15:57` (photo 57 instead of 15). Your 🔧 request lists them ("🗑️ 15, 📷 15→57").
- **"Same spot" when replacing:** the bot keeps the old layout and puts the new photo in the old one's spot (cut to fit, never through a face, or shown a bit smaller). If anything else changed at the same time (title, design, more photos), the album is planned again.
- **The words** are in `server/texts/he.json` (`ask_photo_number`, `photo_…`, `ask_address`, `address_…`, `payment…`, `paid_customer`, `owner_payment`, `owner_paid`).
- **Code:** `server/conversation.py` (the new steps), `server/orders.py`, `server/owner_approval.py`, `album/preview.py` (the numbers), `album/build_album.py` + `album/layout.py` (replace/remove). Tests: `tests/test_conversation.py`, `tests/test_owner_approval.py`, `tests/test_layout.py`, `tests/test_preview.py`.
