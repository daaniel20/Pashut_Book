# Task 3.2: Photo numbers, replace or remove a photo, address, payment and help

**Time:** about 15 minutes. **Cost:** ₪0.
**Do it on:** Supabase (in the browser), the Mac Mini and your phone.

**Why:** a full order should run from start to finish: the customer can fix a photo they don't like, sends a shipping address, and pays.

**What you get at the end:**
1. **A number on every photo** in the preview page (a dark circle with a bold white number in the photo's top corner; made about twice as big after your phone test, so it reads on a phone). It is the photo's own number, the order it was sent in, so it never changes when the album is built again. The cover has no number. 🔒 The numbers are on the preview only, **never printed**.
2. **"📷 להחליף/להסיר תמונה"** at the top of the **✏️ לשנות משהו** list (only once the customer has seen the numbers):
   - "איזו תמונה?" → the customer types the number (e.g. `15`). A number that isn't in the album gets "לא מצאנו…".
   - **[📷 להחליף] [🗑️ להסיר] [↩️ בלי שינוי]** (the same "no change" button as in task 1.2b: nothing is built again)
   - **The album is never planned again for these changes** (fixed after your phone test, Nov 26, 2026, where replacing photo 8 and removing photo 10 shuffled every page). The bot starts from the album exactly as the customer saw it:
     - **Replace** → "send the new photo" (with a tip: the same way round, upright or sideways, fits best) → it goes into **the same spot**. If its shape doesn't fit that spot (e.g. a wide photo where an upright one was), **only that page** is laid out again. The customer chose it, so it's always used.
     - **Remove** → **only that page** is laid out again with its other photos. If it was alone on its page, that page goes and the pages after it move up one page. A photo alone on its page can't be removed if the album would get shorter than its minimum length: the bot offers to replace it instead.
     - The whole album is planned again only for a new design, added photos or another cover photo.
   - Then the usual summary, and the new album link comes.
3. **After you tap ✅ לאשר:**
   - the customer gets "מוכן להדפסה!" and the shipping questions, **one at a time** (changed after your phone test: one message let mistakes through): 👤 full name → 🏠 street and house number (must have a number) → 🏙️ city (letters only) → 📱 the courier's phone: **[📱 המספר הזה]** (their WhatsApp number in one tap) or a typed Israeli mobile number,
   - each question shows what was just saved ("✅ שם: …") with a **[✏️ לתקן …]** button for it; after fixing, they carry on where they were,
   - at the end a summary of all four: **[✅ נכון] [✏️ לתקן]** ("לתקן" opens a list: which detail),
   - **✅ נכון** → the customer sees the price ("₪180 (אלבום ₪150 + משלוח ₪30)") and "we'll send you a Bit payment request to this WhatsApp number",
   - **you** get 🔧 "לגבות תשלום" with the amount, the customer's **full** phone number and the address, and a **[💰 התקבל תשלום]** button.
4. **You send the Bit request by hand**, from your own Bit app, to that number. When the money arrives, tap **💰 התקבל תשלום** → the customer gets "התשלום התקבל… האלבום יוצא להדפסה", and you get the print files' folder.
5. After approval the customer **can't change the album** any more: "change something" says it's approved and, for something important, to write **עזרה**.
6. **Limits on everything** (CLAUDE.md rules 11 and 12), so nobody can abuse the bot:
   - **5 times per album for each kind of change**: title, design, cover photo, adding photos, replacing/removing photos, and sending another cover photo after the bot said it doesn't fit. Tapping "↩️ בלי שינוי" doesn't count. At the limit: "אפשר … עד 5 פעמים באלבום, וכבר הגעתם לזה 🙂 האלבום נשאר כמו שהוא." with the album's buttons.
   - **3 new albums per phone number per day** (counted from Supabase, cancelled ones included). The 4th "אלבום חדש" that day gets a polite "try tomorrow", and the current album stays.
   - **3 messages to you per customer per day** (after "love it", while paying, and "עזרה" together). After that: "קיבלנו את ההודעות שלכם…" and nothing more reaches you.
   - The bot no longer says "write to us" anywhere: "עזרה" (once a day) is the only way to reach you.
7. **"עזרה" (help)** at any time: the customer types **עזרה** → "כתבו כאן את השאלה שלכם…" → their next **typed** message comes to you with a **[💬 לענות]** button. Your answer goes to them as is, without buttons. Answering is your choice.
   - **Text only:** photos never come to your phone.
   - **Once a day per customer:** a second "עזרה" the same day gets "קיבלנו את ההודעה הקודמת שלכם…" and nothing is passed on.
   - Long messages are cut at about 500 letters.
   - Later, a separate customer-service number will take these instead of your private phone.

> **Words you'll see**
> - **Column:** one piece of information in every row of a Supabase table (like a column in a spreadsheet). This task adds three to `orders`.
> - **Status:** the order's stage in Supabase: `collecting` → `ready` (album built) → `approved` (you approved: address and payment) → `paid`. Or `cancelled`.

## Step 4: Try it on your phone

Your phone can be both the customer and the owner (as in task 3.1).

1. Make a small album and open the preview link. Every photo inside has a small number.
2. Tap **✏️ לשנות משהו** → **📷 להחליף/להסיר תמונה** → type a number from the preview → **📷 להחליף** → send a new photo. In the new link, the new photo is in the same place (with its own new number), and the other pages didn't change.
3. Do it again with **🗑️ להסיר**: that photo is gone.
4. Tap **😍 אהבתי!**, then **✅ לאשר** on the 🔧 request.
5. As the customer, answer the four shipping questions (try a mistake: a street without a number, then **✏️ לתקן** on one detail), then **✅ נכון**.
6. You get 🔧 **לגבות תשלום** with ₪180 and your number. (In a real order: send the Bit request now.) Tap **💰 התקבל תשלום**.
7. Type **עזרה**, then a question → it comes to you with **💬 לענות**. Tap it and type an answer → it arrives as a customer message. Type **עזרה** again → "we got your earlier message" (once a day).
8. In Supabase → **Table Editor** → `orders`: the newest row has `status` = `paid`, `ship_name`, `ship_street`, `ship_city`, `ship_phone`, `price` = 180 and `paid_at`.

To see what happened: `grep -i "paid\|payment\|album" ~/Library/Logs/pashut-book/bot.log | tail -10`

Try it without a phone too: `python3 server/try_conversation.py` (new commands: `/previews`, `/approve`, `/paid`).

## Good to know

- **Photos that arrive after the summary** (a phone still uploading big HD photos or videos; your test, Nov 26, 2026: 4 photos came 4 minutes late): the bot stays quiet while they arrive, then sends one message, e.g. "קיבלנו עוד 4 תמונות (בסך הכול 89 תמונות)", and builds the album again once. Videos after the summary get no answer, and "האלבום בהכנה" is said only once per album.

- **The daily limits (help, messages to you) are per album:** if a customer starts a new album the same day, they can ask once more (the 3-new-albums-a-day limit keeps this small).
- **The 24-hour window** (see guide 3.1): the "approved" message, the payment request and "payment received" reach the customer only within 24 hours of their last message. Approve, and collect, within a day. Typing **אישורים** now also resends orders waiting for payment.
- **Why Bit by hand:** no payment company yet (no business registration, fees). A payment link (Grow / PayPlus / Cardcom) comes in Stage 4. The customer never types card details in WhatsApp.
- **Privacy:** the shipping details are kept in Supabase only (for shipping), never in the bot log. Delete it with the order's photos later (task 3.4 deletes photos after delivery).
- **The customer's photo changes** are in the order's notes: `removed:15` (photo 15 left out) and `replaced:15:57` (photo 57 instead of 15). Your 🔧 request lists them ("🗑️ 15, 📷 15→57").
- **"Same spot" when replacing:** the bot keeps the old layout and puts the new photo in the old one's spot (cut to fit, never through a face, or shown a bit smaller). If anything else changed at the same time (title, design, more photos), the album is planned again.
- **The words** are in `server/texts/he.json` (`ask_photo_number`, `photo_…`, `ask_ship_…`, `ship_…`, `button_fix_…`, `fix_row_…`, `address_check`, `payment…`, `paid_customer`, `owner_payment`, `owner_paid`, `help_…`, `owner_help`, `owner_write_help`).
- **Code:** `server/conversation.py` (the new steps), `server/orders.py`, `server/owner_approval.py`, `album/preview.py` (the numbers), `album/build_album.py` + `album/layout.py` (replace/remove). Tests: `tests/test_conversation.py`, `tests/test_owner_approval.py`, `tests/test_layout.py`, `tests/test_preview.py`.
