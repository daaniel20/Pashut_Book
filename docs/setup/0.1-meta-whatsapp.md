# Task 0.1: Meta account + free WhatsApp test number

**Time:** about 30–45 minutes. **Cost:** ₪0.
**Do it on:** a computer (the Mac Mini is fine), with your phone nearby.

> ⚠️ **Learned on the first try (Nov 2026):** Meta now blocks a **brand-new, unverified business portfolio** from sending any messages, **even from the free test number** (error 131031 "Business Account locked"; WhatsApp Manager says "Account Restricted: verify your business"). Receiving still works. So you need either a portfolio that is **already verified**, or **Business Verification** (Step 8), before the test message can arrive.

**What you get at the end:** a free WhatsApp "test number" that belongs to your Meta app. It can send messages to **up to 5 phone numbers** that you register (your phone and a few family members' phones). This is enough to build and test the bot. Real customers will need a real business number later (task 0.4).

> **Words you'll see**
> - **Meta for Developers:** Meta's website for people who build things on Facebook/WhatsApp.
> - **App:** a "container" in Meta that holds your WhatsApp settings. It's not an app in the App Store, and nobody downloads it.
> - **Business portfolio** (sometimes called "Business Manager"): Meta's folder for your business. The app and the WhatsApp number belong to it.
> - **Access token:** a long password that lets our server send WhatsApp messages. **Secret.**

## Step 1: Prepare your Facebook account (5 min)

1. Use your **personal Facebook account** (Meta requires one). Nobody will see it; customers see only the business name.
2. Turn on **two-factor authentication** (a code by SMS or app at login): Facebook → Settings → Accounts Center → Password and security → Two-factor authentication. Meta often blocks developer features without it, and it protects the business.

## Step 2: Register as a Meta developer (5 min)

1. Go to **developers.facebook.com** and click **Get Started** (or **Log in**).
2. Accept the terms, and confirm your phone number and email when asked.
3. For "role", pick anything close (for example "Developer" or "Entrepreneur"). It doesn't matter.

## Step 3: Create the app (5 min)

1. Click **My Apps** → **Create App**.
2. **App name:** `Pashut Album`. Meta doesn't allow the word "Book" in app names. Customers never see this name. **Email:** your email.
3. **Use case:** choose **"Connect with customers through WhatsApp"** (the wording may differ slightly; pick the WhatsApp one).
4. **Business portfolio:** if asked, create a new one called **Pashut Book**. (If you already have one, you can use it.)
5. Click through to finish. Meta may ask for your Facebook password again.

## Step 4: Get the test number (2 min)

1. In the app's left menu, open **WhatsApp → Step 1. Try it out** (older name: **API Setup**). Accept the WhatsApp terms if asked.
2. Meta creates a **test number** automatically. You'll see it in the **From** field (it's usually a US number starting with +1 555…).
3. On the same page you'll see two IDs. Copy them into a note on your computer (they are **not** secret, but keep them handy):
   - **Phone number ID**
   - **WhatsApp Business Account ID** (labeled **Messaging account ID**)

## Step 5: Add your phone as a recipient (3 min)

1. On the same page, in the **To** field, click **Manage phone number list** → **Add phone number**.
2. Enter your number in international format: **+972** and then your number **without the first 0**. For example, `050-123-4567` becomes `+972 50 123 4567`.
3. WhatsApp on your phone gets a **code**. Type it into Meta's page.
4. You can add up to 5 numbers. For now, add **only yours**. Add family members later when we test the bot.

## Step 6: Send the first test message (2 min)

1. Click **Generate access token**. This token works for **24 hours only**. That's fine for today. If Meta asks which WhatsApp accounts to give access to, choose **current accounts only** and tick only the **Test** account.
2. In **To**, choose your phone number.
3. Click **Send message**. Meta sends a ready-made test message (now "Order Confirmation"; it used to be "Hello World").
4. ✅ Your phone should get it in WhatsApp from the test number within a few seconds. If not, open the **Check test webhooks** panel on the same page: a "failed" status with error **131031** means the business needs verification (Step 8).
5. **Reply "Hi"** to it. (Replying opens a 24-hour window where the bot is allowed to send you normal messages. You'll learn more about this rule in Stage 1.)
6. Save the test number in your phone's contacts as **"Pashut Book TEST"**.

> **Where do I see my "Hi"?** The **Check test webhooks** panel on the Try-it-out page shows incoming messages for testing. For the real bot, our server receives them through a **webhook** (an automatic notice from Meta to our server: "a new message arrived"), set up in tasks 0.2 and 1.1.

## Step 7: Keep the secrets safe

- **Never paste the access token** into a chat (including this one), an email, or a document. If it leaks, someone could send messages in your business's name.
- You don't need to save today's token anywhere; it expires in 24 hours.
- In task 1.1 we'll create a **permanent** token and put it in a `.env` file on the Mac Mini (a private settings file that's never uploaded to GitHub). The file `.env.example` in this project shows which values will go there.

## Step 8: Business Verification (if sending is blocked)

1. Open **WhatsApp Manager** → **Verify your business in Security Center** (or business settings → **Security Center**).
2. Meta asks for the **legal business name, address and phone**, and an **official document** (for example, an עוסק פטור/מורשה certificate).
3. Meta usually answers within a few days to about 2 weeks.

**Shortcut (what we did):** an **older** portfolio you own may not have this restriction, even if it was never verified (its Security Center may say "Your organization does not need to be verified"). Create a separate test app under it, just for testing with your own phones. Don't use a portfolio that belongs to an employer or someone else. Move to a Pashut Book portfolio before real customers (task 0.4).

## Something went wrong?

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Create App" is greyed out or Meta asks to verify your account | Finish two-factor authentication (Step 1), log out and in again, wait an hour, and try again. |
| The verification code doesn't arrive | Check the number format (+972, no leading 0). Make sure WhatsApp is installed on that number. Try "Resend". |
| Status "failed", error **131031 Business Account locked** | The business portfolio isn't verified. See Step 8. |
| "Send message" gives an error about the recipient | The number isn't verified yet (Step 5), or a different number is selected in **To**. |
| Error about the token | It expired (24 hours). Click **Generate access token** again. |
| The menus look different from this guide | Meta changes its website often. Look for the same words (WhatsApp, API Setup, To, From). Send me a screenshot **with the token hidden** and I'll guide you. |
