# Task 1.3: Collect and store photos

> ✅ **Done on Nov 22, 2026.** 57 photos sent from the owner's phone: 57 counted, 57 saved.

**Time:** about 15 minutes. **Cost:** ₪0 (Supabase free plan).
**Do it on:** the Mac Mini, with your phone nearby.

**What you get at the end:**
- Every photo a customer sends is **saved** in a private storage area in Supabase, numbered in the order it arrived (001, 002, …).
- The bot **remembers each customer** even after a restart (of the bot or the Mac).
- The 100-photo limit is enforced: photos after the limit are not saved.

> **Words you'll see**
> - **Table:** like a spreadsheet inside Supabase. `orders` has one row per album; `photos` has one row per saved photo.
> - **Bucket:** a folder for files in Supabase. Ours is called `photos` and is **private**: no photo has a public link. Only the bot (with the secret key) can open it.

## Good to know

- **Starting over** (writing **אלבום חדש**) before finishing an order cancels it, and its photos are **deleted** a minute later. A finished order is kept.
- **Free plan space:** 1 GB of files. An HD photo is about 1–4 MB, so roughly 5–10 albums at a time. Photos of delivered albums will be deleted automatically (a later task), so this is enough to start. If we need more, Supabase Pro is $25/month (100 GB).
- **If a photo fails to save** (for example, the internet drops), the bot tries 3 times. If it still fails, the log says "❌ Could not save photo…" and `check_supabase.py` shows ⏳ for that order.
- **If Supabase is down,** the customer gets "אופס, יש לנו תקלה טכנית קטנה" (at most once every 10 minutes) and can try again later.
- **The log** shows only order numbers (like `order 3f2a9c1b`) and photo numbers. Never photos, names or full phone numbers.
