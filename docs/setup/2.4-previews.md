# Task 2.4: Watermarked previews in WhatsApp

**Time:** about 15 minutes. **Cost:** ₪0 (sending images in a conversation the customer started is free).
**Do it on:** the Mac Mini and your phone.

**What you get at the end:**
- When the bot finishes building an album, the customer gets the album **in WhatsApp**, as small images with a **PREVIEW** mark:
  1. a short message ("your album is ready…"),
  2. the cover, page 1, then **two pages per image**, like an open book (pages 2–3, 4–5 …), then the last page. Each image has the page numbers under the pages and a caption ("עמודים 2–3"), so the customer can say "page 5: …",
  3. a last message with two buttons: **[😍 אהבתי!]** and **[✏️ לשנות משהו]**.
- "אהבתי" gets a short thank-you ("we'll be in touch about payment and shipping"). Payment itself is task 3.2.
- "לשנות משהו" opens the same list as the summary (title, design, cover photo, add photos). After the change, the album is built again and the **new** previews are sent.

> 🔒 **Why the customer can't print them:** each page is only 700 pixels (about 90 DPI on a 20 cm page; print needs 300), and the PREVIEW mark crosses every page three times. The print PDF never leaves the Mac.

> **Words you'll see**
> - **Upload:** the bot first gives each image to Meta privately (it gets a number, not a public web address), then sends it. Meta deletes these copies by itself after 30 days.
> - **Spread:** two pages side by side, as you see them when the book is open.

## Step 2: Try it on your phone

1. Write **אלבום חדש** to the test number and go through the whole conversation (design, cover photo, photos, title).
2. At the summary, wait. After the album is built (about a minute for 40 photos), you get the message, the images, and the two buttons.
3. Check: are the images in order? Can you read the page numbers? Is the mark visible but not annoying?
4. Tap **✏️ לשנות משהו**, change the title, and check that new previews arrive with the new title.
5. Tap **😍 אהבתי!**.

You can also look at the images on the Mac without a phone: they are in the album's `preview` folder. The build command prints the line to open it:

```bash
cd ~/pashut-book && .venv/bin/python album/build_album.py --order latest
```

> Building by hand like this does **not** send anything to the customer. Only the bot sends previews.

## Good to know

- **Order of the images:** WhatsApp doesn't promise that images arrive in the order they were sent, so the bot waits 1.5 seconds between them. If they still arrive mixed up, tell Claude (the setting is `PREVIEW_PAUSE_SECONDS` in `server/album_job.py`).
- **If the customer is in the middle of a change** (for example typing a new title) when the album is ready, the previews wait. The new album is built when they finish, and its previews are sent then.
- **If one image fails to send**, the bot stops and writes it in the log (`~/Library/Logs/pashut-book/bot.log`). Build and check by hand, then write to the customer yourself.
- **The 24-hour rule:** WhatsApp lets a business send free messages only within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Today the album is built right after the summary, so this is fine. If an album is ever sent much later (for example after a manual fix), it will need an approved message template (task 3.x).
- **How the mark looks** is set at the top of `album/preview.py` (`MARK`, `MARK_OPACITY`, `PAGE_PIXELS`).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The album is built (log says ✅) but no images arrive | Look in the log for "Could not send a message". A "Meta error" there explains why (for example an expired token: run `python3 server/check_whatsapp.py`). |
| Log says "previews wait" | The customer was changing something. The previews come after they finish. |
| Images arrive out of order | Raise `PREVIEW_PAUSE_SECONDS` in `server/album_job.py`, restart the bot. |
