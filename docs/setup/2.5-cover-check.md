# Task 2.5: Check the cover photo when it arrives

**Time:** about 10 minutes. **Cost:** ₪0.
**Do it on:** the Mac Mini and your phone.

**Why:** your first cover printed at only 146 DPI (a wide photo, sent without HD, cut to the square cover). Print needs about 300; under 150 it looks blurry. Now the customer hears about it within seconds, while the phone is still in their hand.

**What you get at the end:**
1. The customer sends the cover photo and right away gets: "קיבלנו את התמונה 👍 בודקים שהיא מתאימה לכריכה, זה ייקח כמה שניות…"
2. The bot waits for the photo to be saved, then checks it: is it big enough for an edge-to-edge cover (at least 150 DPI after cutting it to the cover's square shape), and is it sharp?
3. **Good** (at least 200 DPI and sharp) → the usual "איזו תמונה יפה לכריכה! 😍" and the customer goes on sending photos.
4. **Too small or blurry** → one message with two buttons:
   - **[📷 לשלוח תמונה אחרת]** (or **[📷 לשלוח שוב ב-HD]** when the photo came without HD; the message then explains how to send in HD).
   - **[להמשיך עם התמונה]** → the album shows this photo **smaller** on the cover (whole, with white around it and the title under it), so it stays sharp. The album page for you (`album.html`) shows a ⚠️ note about it.

The same check also works when the customer changes the cover photo from the summary.

> **Words you'll see**
> - **DPI** (dots per inch): how many photo pixels fill each inch of paper. 300 = perfect, under 150 = blurry. A 20 cm cover is about 8 inches, so it needs at least ~1,200 pixels across *after* cutting.
> - **Why upright photos are better:** the cover is square. A wide photo loses its sides, so fewer pixels are left. Without HD, an upright photo still gets 195 DPI (fine), a wide 16:9 photo only 146 (too small).

## Step 2: Try it on your phone

1. Write **אלבום חדש**, choose a design.
2. Send any photo **without HD** as the cover. You should see "בודקים…", and a few seconds later the "send it again in HD" message with the two buttons.
   > Changed after the owner's test (Nov 24, 2026): photos without HD passed at 195 DPI, so the check now asks for HD under **200 DPI** (`printer.GOOD_DPI`), which means every cover sent without HD. Between 150 and 200 the photo still prints fine full size, so "continue" keeps it full size; only under 150 (e.g. a very wide photo without HD) is it shown smaller. To see each check's DPI: `grep -i "cover" ~/Library/Logs/pashut-book/bot.log | tail -5`
3. Tap **📷 לשלוח שוב ב-HD** and send the same photo **with HD**. This time: "איזו תמונה יפה לכריכה! 😍".
4. Optional: start again, send the small photo, tap **להמשיך עם התמונה**, finish the order, and look at the cover in the preview link: the photo is smaller, with the title under it.

You can also check any photo on the Mac by hand:

```bash
cd ~/pashut-book && .venv/bin/python album/check_cover.py ~/Desktop/photo.jpg
```

## Good to know

- **The limits** are in `album/printer.py` (`GOOD_DPI` = 200: under it, ask for HD or another photo; `LOW_DPI` = 150: under it, the cover is shown smaller) and `album/quality.py` (`BLURRY_BELOW`). Only clearly blurry photos are flagged (not "a bit soft"), so customers aren't bothered for nothing.
- **Photos sent while the check runs** are counted as album photos, as usual.
- **If the check can't run** (album tools missing, Supabase slow), the photo is simply accepted and the log says why. The album itself never prints a too-small cover edge to edge: it shows it smaller, and you approve every album anyway.
- **Photos sent before choosing a design** (the first becomes the cover) are not checked right away; the album still shows a too-small one smaller, with a note for you.
- **The words** are in `server/texts/he.json` (`cover_checking`, `cover_problem_…`, `button_cover_…`, `cover_kept`).
- **Try it without a phone:** `python3 server/try_conversation.py`, then `/photo small` or `/photo blurry` for the cover.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "בודקים…" and then nothing | Look in the log (`~/Library/Logs/pashut-book/bot.log`) for "Cover check". If it says the album tools are missing, see `docs/setup/2.1-quality-check.md`, step 1. Writing anything to the bot after a restart starts the check again. |
| A good photo is called "too small" | Run `album/check_cover.py` on it and see the DPI. A wide photo without HD really is too small for the square cover. |
| A blurry photo was not caught | Same as in task 2.1: very contrasty photos can still score "sharp". Your approval before printing is the safety net. |
