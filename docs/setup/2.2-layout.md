# Task 2.2: Automatic album layout

**Time:** about 30 minutes. **Cost:** ₪0.
**Do it on:** the Mac Mini, with your phone nearby.

**What you get at the end:**
- The customer **chooses the cover photo** first, then sends the rest. The summary has a button to change it.
- The computer designs a **whole album by itself**: an edge-to-edge cover with the title, then 20–40 pages (about 1 page for every 2.5 photos) with 1 to 4 photos each, in one of the 2 designs (Classic / Colorful).
- Lower-quality photos go **only in small spots**. Blurry photos and duplicates are left out.
- The bot tells the customer about photo problems in **one short message**, only when there is a problem, for example: *"3 תמונות מטושטשות ו-2 תמונות כפולות לא נכנסו לאלבום"*.
- If **most photos came without HD**, the bot asks, before building the album, whether to send them again in HD, with 2 buttons: **[איך שולחים ב-HD?] [להמשיך בכל זאת]**.
- The album is saved **on the Mac only**, as a page you open to look through it like a book. The customer doesn't see it yet: previews are task 2.4, your approval is task 3.1.

> ⚠️ **The page size and the look are a first guess.** The printer (S.2) and the 2 templates (S.3) aren't decided yet, so the album is a 20×20 cm square with 20 to 40 pages. Everything about the look is in one settings file, `album/designs.py` (page size, margins, colours, fonts, page layouts). When the printer sends its specifications, we change it there and the whole album adapts.

> **Words you'll see**
> - **Layout:** the plan of which photo goes on which page, and where.
> - **Template:** a ready-made page plan with spots for photos (for example "1 big photo + 2 small").
> - **Spread:** two pages side by side, as you see them when the book is open.

What's new:
- **The customer chooses the cover photo.** After choosing a design, the bot asks for 1 photo for the cover, then for the rest ("40 to 100 photos give the nicest album"). The summary now has 3 buttons: change the title, the design, or the **cover photo**.
- **Edge-to-edge cover**, with the title on a soft band at the bottom, or at the top if faces are low in the photo. If a photo can't fill the cover without cutting a face (for example a very wide photo), it's shown smaller with the title under it, as before.

You can always build an order's album by hand. It uses the design and title the customer chose:

```bash

```

(Or give the order's first 8 characters instead of `latest`.) The bot doesn't build albums of orders that were finished while it was off or restarting; use this command for them.

## Good to know

- **How it decides:** about 2–3 photos per page, at least 24 pages, an even number of pages (an empty page is added at the end if needed). It mixes busy pages with single big photos, and in Classic, now and then a photo covers a whole page edge to edge. For each page it picks the template that cuts the least off the photos. Photos with people are cut at most 15% and never through a face (it keeps room above heads for hair and raised hands); otherwise the whole photo is shown smaller.
- **Order:** photos keep the order they were sent (or the date taken, when the photos still have it). On a page they may swap places so tall photos get tall spots.
- **HD question:** asked when at least half the photos arrived at exactly 2048 pixels (what WhatsApp sends without HD). Asked only once per order.
- **Fewer than about 24 photos:** the album is shorter than the minimum, and the album page shows a ⚠️ note for you.
- **The cover** uses the first good photo, which also appears inside the album.
- **Privacy:** the album pages contain the customer's photos, so they stay in the `output` folder on the Mac (never uploaded to GitHub). Automatic deletion after delivery is task 3.3. To delete test albums now: `rm -r ~/pashut-book/output/albums`
- **Face finder:** OpenCV with the "YuNet" model (a small free file, MIT licence, in `album/models`). It runs only on the Mac and only finds where faces are. The same model is used by the Rega album maker (see `docs/research-notes.md`).
- **Fonts:** Frank Ruhl Libre (Classic) and Rubik (Colorful), free Google fonts for Hebrew. Their licences are in `album/fonts`.
- **For task 2.3:** each album folder has `layout.json`, the plan of every page in millimetres. The print PDF will draw the same plan at print quality.
